hashfootery is back

A score prediction game for Premier League football. Predict the score of each match to earn points - 3 for an exact score, 1 for the correct result. At the start of the season you also make a set of bonus predictions that score points as the season unfolds.

Make your predictions →

How to play

3
points for predicting the exact score
1
point for the correct result (win/draw/loss)
0
points if you get the result wrong

Submitting via the site

Head to the Predict page, enter your name, and fill in your score predictions before each match kicks off. Predictions lock 30 minutes before kick-off.

Submitting via Bluesky

Register your Bluesky handle on the Predict page, then post your prediction in this format:

#hashfootery Arsenal 2-1 Chelsea

The @hashfooterybot will reply to confirm your prediction has been logged. Bluesky predictions also lock 30 minutes before kick-off. You can use common abbreviations - Spurs, Man City, Brighton, Forest etc.

Season predictions

Before the first match of the season kicks off, you can make a set of bonus predictions. These lock at GW1 kick-off and score points as results are confirmed throughout the season.

League positions

Premier League champions — 10pts
Top 4 in order — 4pts exact, 2pts right team wrong spot
Two relegated teams — 5pts each

Individual awards

Golden Boot — 8pts
Most assists — 6pts
First hat-trick scorer — 6pts

Chaos predictions

First manager sacked — 6pts
First red card — 5pts (3pts if not first)
Spurs final position — 5pts

Deadlines & updates

Each match prediction locks 30 minutes before kick-off. Season predictions lock when the first match of the season kicks off. You can update any prediction before its deadline by submitting again on the site or posting a new prediction on Bluesky - it will override your previous entry.

Standings

The standings table shows total points across all match and season predictions combined, with a breakdown of each. Players are ranked by total points - exact scores are the tiebreaker.
