hashfootery is back

The score prediction game for football fans. Predict the score of Premier League matches - tweet your pick with #hashfootery or enter it on the site. 3 points for the exact score, 1 point for the correct result.

Make your predictions →

How to play

3
points for predicting the exact score
1
point for the correct result (win/draw/loss)
0
points if you get the outcome wrong

Submitting via the site

Head to the Predict page, enter your name, and fill in your score predictions before each match kicks off. Predictions lock 30 minutes before kickoff.

Submitting via Bluesky

Register your Bluesky handle on the Predict page, then post a prediction in this format:

#hashfootery Arsenal 2-1 Chelsea

The @hashfooterybot will reply to confirm your prediction has been logged. Bluesky posts also lock 30 minutes before kickoff.
